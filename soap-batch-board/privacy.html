Soap Batch Board Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Soap Batch Board is a local-first production log for user-entered recipe references, batches, cure plans, packaging, labels, reminders, and backups.

Information stored on your device

Batch and recipe-reference names, ingredient reference values, dates, cure plans, packaging runs, labels, notes, reminders, settings, and backups are stored in the app's private storage. The current release does not require an account or upload this content to a WittyApps server.

Analytics and crash reports

Amplitude may process privacy-limited app events, an app-instance identifier, app version, platform, locale, and coarse device information. Sentry may process crash diagnostics and technical device or operating-system information. These services are configured not to receive formula values, ingredient data, batch names, dates, notes, labels, reminder content, or backup contents.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes transactions. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, product and entitlement status, purchase history, and limited app or device information needed to provide and restore access. WittyApps does not receive full payment-card details.

Reminders, backups, and sharing

Reminders are scheduled locally after you opt in. Backup, restore, label export, and sharing actions occur only when requested; destination services handle selected files under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Control notification permission.
  • Export, restore, or delete local records using in-app controls.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling, subject to device backup settings.
  • Restore eligible purchases through the store.

Retention and children

Production records remain on your device until deleted. Technical providers retain limited data according to their service settings and policies. The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. Questions or privacy requests: cleverlywittyapps@gmail.com.
