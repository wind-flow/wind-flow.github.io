Weekly Flock Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Weekly Flock Pulse is a local-first journal for flock check-ins, egg counts, feed spending, observations, notes, reminders, trends, and exports.

Information stored on your device

Flock names, counts, egg counts, dates, feed quantities and costs, currencies, observations, notes, reminders, settings, and history are stored in the app's private storage. The current release does not require an account or upload flock content to a WittyApps server.

Analytics and crash reports

Amplitude may process privacy-limited app events, an app-instance identifier, app version, platform, locale, and coarse device information. Sentry may process crash diagnostics and technical device or operating-system information. These services are configured not to receive flock names, counts, egg counts, feed quantities or costs, currencies, dates, observations, notes, reminder content, or exports.

Purchases

Apple or Google processes transactions. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, product and entitlement status, purchase history, and limited app or device information needed to provide and restore access. WittyApps does not receive full payment-card details.

Reminders and exports

Weekly reminders are scheduled locally only after you opt in. CSV, PDF, and sharing actions occur only when requested; selected destination services then handle the exported file under their own policies.

Your choices

  • Control notification permission.
  • Edit, export, or delete local journal records using in-app controls.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling, subject to device backup settings.
  • Restore eligible purchases through the store.

Retention and children

Journal records remain on your device until deleted. Technical providers retain limited data according to their service settings and policies. The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. Questions or privacy requests: cleverlywittyapps@gmail.com.
