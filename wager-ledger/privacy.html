Wager Ledger
Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Wager Ledger is a local gambling diary for Android published by WittyApps. It helps you keep session records and evidence for your own tax records. This policy explains what the app processes.

1. Records stored on your device

Sessions, establishment names and addresses, names of other people present, amounts, notes, and evidence photos are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. There is no account, no WittyApps server, and no automatic cloud sync.

Photos are taken with the camera or chosen from your gallery only when you add evidence, and the app keeps its own copy in private storage.

App storage is excluded from Android system backup and device-to-device transfer. To move your records to a new device, create a backup file in the app and restore it on the new device.

If you turn on App lock, the app uses your device's biometric or screen-lock prompt. Fingerprint, face, and device passcode data are handled by the operating system; the app receives only whether authentication succeeded.

2. Usage analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They may process an app-instance identifier, device and OS information, language, app version, screen views, event times, and technical crash details. Events the app adds are limited to screen names, button actions, and success or failure states.

Amounts, establishment names, people's names, notes, file names, and photos are never sent to analytics or crash reporting. The app does not use an advertising identifier and shows no ads.

3. Exports and backups you share

PDF, CSV, and backup files leave your device only when you share or save them through Android's share sheet or file picker. The app or person receiving them then holds that copy under their own policies. Backup files contain all records and photos and are not encrypted, so share them only with places you trust.

4. Retention and deletion

Records and photos stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. WittyApps cannot see or recover them. Files you exported or backed up elsewhere remain until you delete them.

Analytics and crash data are kept according to the Firebase project's settings and Google's policies; Crashlytics begins deleting crash data and related identifiers after 90 days.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics — Firebase privacy information
  • Google Play — distribution and updates

These providers may process data on servers outside your country.

6. Children

Wager Ledger is intended for adults who gamble legally and is not directed at children.

7. Not tax advice

Wager Ledger is a record-keeping aid. It does not provide tax, legal, or financial advice. Consult a tax professional about your situation.

8. Contact and changes

If our processing changes, we will update this page. For privacy questions or requests, email cleverlywittyapps@gmail.com. You do not need to send your actual gambling records to get support.
