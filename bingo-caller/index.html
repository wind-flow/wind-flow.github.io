Bingo Caller Support

Help for Bingo Caller: 75-ball and 90-ball bingo calling for hosts, gatherings, and small events.

How to get help

For questions, bug reports, purchase restoration, or accessibility help, email us. Please include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include payment details or other sensitive information.

Email Bingo Caller Support

Common questions

  • Calls and game progress: The current game and settings are saved locally on your device. Use Undo to return the latest called number to the draw.
  • Speech: Text-to-speech can be enabled or disabled in Settings. The app remains usable with speech turned off or unavailable.
  • External display: Connect a supported external display, then use the external-display help in Settings. The audience board shows the current number, recent calls, full board, and remaining count.
  • Remove Ads purchase: Open Settings and choose Remove Ads. If you already purchased it, use Restore Purchases on the same screen. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details.

Privacy

Bingo Caller does not require an account. Game state and preferences are stored on your device. Read the Privacy Policy for details about optional advertising, analytics, crash diagnostics, and purchases.
