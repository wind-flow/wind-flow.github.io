Photo Sweep Support

Photo Sweep helps you review similar photos, large videos and monthly groups, then choose items to remove with system confirmation. Analysis stays on your device.

Start a review

Allow photo access, review a category and mark individual items. The app never removes media automatically. Confirm the selection and then approve the operating system prompt. Free use allows 50 successful removals per local day; cancellation or failure does not spend the allowance.

Recover items or change permission

On iOS, check Photos → Recently Deleted. On Android, check your gallery’s trash. Recovery depends on the operating system and gallery. You can change photo access in system Settings. Cloud-only media may be unavailable until stored locally.

Pro subscriptions

Weekly and yearly Pro remove ads and the daily removal limit while active. Use Settings to purchase, restore or manage your subscription. Cancel renewal through your store account; uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.
