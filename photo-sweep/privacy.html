Photo Sweep Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026. Operator: WittyApps (위티앱스), South Korea. Contact: cleverlywittyapps@gmail.com.

This policy applies to Photo Sweep, package and bundle identifier com.wittyapps.photosweep. Photo Sweep helps you review and clean up your device photo library. It has no separate user account.

Photos and videos stay on your device

With your permission, Photo Sweep reads your photo library to show thumbnails, dates, dimensions, media types, video duration and available size estimates. Similarity analysis runs on your device. We do not upload your photos, videos, thumbnails, photo identifiers, selections or analysis results to our servers, Firebase or AdMob. An item available only in cloud storage may remain unavailable until your operating system makes it available locally.

Nothing is deleted automatically. You select items and approve the operating system's confirmation. On iOS, Photos manages deletion and Recently Deleted recovery; iCloud settings may propagate changes to your other devices. On Android 11 and later, the app requests system media trash and does not substitute permanent deletion. Recovery and retention depend on your operating system and gallery.

Local app information

The app stores scan snapshots and media identifiers, keep/removal choices, unfinished recovery records, settings, language and onboarding state in local app preferences. It also stores a local-date count of successful free deletions and timing used to limit review prompts and full-screen ads. We do not provide account-based storage or synchronization. These preferences are not separately encrypted by Photo Sweep. Device backup behavior follows your operating system and app settings.

Usage analytics and crash diagnostics

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand feature use and improve reliability. These services may process installation identifiers, device and operating system details, app version, language, session and event times, approximate region, app interactions and crash information. Our custom events describe coarse actions, success/failure categories and subscription status. Purchase events may include the store product identifier, localized price and currency. They do not contain your media, photo identifiers or review choices.

We do not set a personal account identifier or send your name or email as analytics properties. There is currently no separate in-app analytics switch. Google describes its processing, international transfers and retention at Firebase privacy and security and Google's privacy policy.

Advertising and choices

Free use includes Google AdMob banner ads and occasional ads after completed cleanup or when reopening the app. An active verified Pro subscription removes ads. Ads are requested only when Google's consent platform permits them. Where required, consent choices appear before advertising and can be revisited through Ad privacy choices in Settings. Declining an optional advertising choice does not disable photo review or the free deletion allowance.

Google's advertising services may collect IP address, approximate location, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Advertising partners may process this information under their own policies and the choices presented in Google's consent form. Photo Sweep does not send photo content or review records to advertisers. You can also use your device's advertising privacy controls. iOS tracking permission is requested when applicable; declining it does not remove core functionality.

Purchases, updates and support

Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds. The app receives product information and verified purchase or subscription status to provide Pro and restore access. We do not receive your card number or banking password.

The app checks for available updates using the platform store or a WittyApps version file hosted on GitHub Pages. Those requests may expose ordinary connection information, such as IP address, to the service provider. GitHub's handling is described in its privacy statement.

If you email support, we process the email address and information you choose to send to respond and resolve the request. Do not send private photos or account passwords; they are not needed for ordinary support.

Retention and deletion

Local review records remain until you clear them through available controls or remove the app's data. Deleting app data does not delete your photo library or cancel a store subscription. System trash and external device backups are managed separately.

Firebase Crashlytics retains crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning deletion. Analytics user/event data follows the project's configured retention period; aggregated reporting has separate retention. Advertising data follows Google and its partners' policies and applicable choices. We retain support correspondence while needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations, then delete it or remove identifying details.

Request data deletion

Photo Sweep (WittyApps) has no user accounts. To request deletion of data associated with your use of Photo Sweep, email cleverlywittyapps@gmail.com with the subject "Photo Sweep data deletion". Include your device platform and, if available, the Firebase installation ID or approximate install date so we can locate related records. We do not ask for photos or passwords.

What can be deleted: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data linked to your app installation, and support emails you sent us. Deletion is completed within 30 days of verifying the request. What is kept: aggregated, non-identifying reports, and data that Google Play, Apple or Google AdMob hold under their own policies (manage those through your Google or Apple account and device ad settings). Data stored only on your device, including review choices and the app's local records, is deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app; your photo library is not affected.

Your rights and contact

You can grant, limit or revoke photo access in system Settings. You can change review choices in the app, manage subscriptions in the store and revisit available advertising privacy choices. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information and to complain to a data protection authority. Send requests to cleverlywittyapps@gmail.com. We may request the minimum information necessary to identify and fulfill your request. We cannot retrieve media or review records held only on your device.

Google, Apple and GitHub may process information outside your country, including in the United States, under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards. Photo Sweep is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We will update this page when our practices change and identify the effective date above.
