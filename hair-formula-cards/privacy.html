Hair Color Formula Book
Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Hair Color Formula Book is a private formula book for hair colorists: client cards, formulas, and reference photos, published by WittyApps for Android and iOS. This policy explains what the app processes.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. There is no account, no WittyApps server, and no automatic cloud sync. Photos or images you choose are read from your camera or gallery only when you use that feature, and are processed on your device.

2. Usage analytics and crash reports

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They may process an anonymous app-instance identifier, device and OS information, language, app version, screen views, event times, and technical crash details. Content you enter is not sent to analytics or crash reporting.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment card details; the store provides purchase status so the app can unlock paid features.

4. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. WittyApps cannot see or recover it. Analytics and crash data are kept according to Firebase and Google policies.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics - Firebase privacy information
  • Google Play and Apple App Store - distribution, updates, and purchases

6. Children

Hair Color Formula Book is not directed at children under 13.

7. Languages

The app is available in English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, German, French, and Brazilian Portuguese.
