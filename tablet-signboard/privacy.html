Privacy Policy — Tablet Signboard

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

Tablet Signboard is an offline message, scoreboard, and notice board for one tablet. It does not require an account or a WittyApps server.

Information stored on your device

Messages, templates, playlists, team names, scores, notices, display settings, language settings, and onboarding status are stored in the app's private local storage. This information stays on your device and is not uploaded by the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, message contents, scores, notices, or other personal information. The current release does not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Network access and updates

The board itself works offline. The app may contact the public Apple App Store or Google Play listing when the operating system or update component checks whether a newer version is available. That check does not send your board contents to WittyApps.

Permissions

Tablet Signboard does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, photo library, or notification access. The app uses screen-awake and full-screen display controls only while you are actively showing a board.

Your choices and deletion

  • Edit or delete messages, playlists, scores, and notices inside the app.
  • Change the language in Settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its local data, subject to your device backup settings.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to cleverlywittyapps@gmail.com.
