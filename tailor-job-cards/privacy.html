Tailor Job Cards Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Tailor Job Cards is a local-first workspace for tailoring clients, measurements, garment jobs, fittings, photos, reminders, and exports. This policy explains how the app handles that information and limited technical data.

Information stored on your device

Client names and contact details, measurement templates and snapshots, job and fitting records, notes, photos, due dates, settings, and local backups are stored in the app's private storage. Workspace records and attachments are encrypted at rest. The app does not require an account and does not upload workspace content to a WittyApps server in the current release.

Analytics and crash reports

Amplitude may process privacy-limited app events, an app-instance identifier, app version, platform, locale, and coarse device information so we can understand whether core workflows succeed. Sentry may process crash diagnostics, app version, operating-system and device information. Events and crash reports are configured not to include client identities, contact details, measurements, notes, photos, exported files, screenshots, or view hierarchy.

Purchases

If paid features are offered, Apple or Google processes the transaction. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, product and entitlement status, purchase history, and limited device or app information needed to provide and restore access. WittyApps does not receive full payment-card details.

Photos, reminders, backups, and exports

Photo access is used only when you select an image for a job. Fitting reminders are scheduled locally after you opt in and use generic text. Backup, PDF, and sharing actions occur only when you request them. Files then leave app protection and are handled by the destination service you select.

Your choices

  • Choose whether to grant photo and notification permission.
  • Export or delete workspace data using in-app controls.
  • Delete local data by uninstalling the app, subject to device backup settings.
  • Restore eligible purchases through the store.

Retention and security

Workspace content remains on your device until you delete it. Technical providers retain their limited data according to their service settings and policies. No system is completely secure, but the app reduces exposure by keeping domain content local and encrypting its workspace.

Children

Tailor Job Cards is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its service providers change. The current version will remain available here with its effective date.
