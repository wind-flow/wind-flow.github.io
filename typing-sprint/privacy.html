Privacy Policy — Typing Sprint

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

Overview

Typing Sprint is a local-first typing practice app published by WittyApps. It does not require an account or sign-in, and WittyApps does not operate a server that receives what you type. Practice results and preferences are stored on your device. The free version shows advertising, and an optional one-time Pro purchase removes advertising.

Information Handled by the App

  • Typed practice text and results: text is processed on your device to calculate speed and accuracy. Typed text is not intentionally sent to WittyApps, Firebase, or AdMob.
  • Local settings and progress: language, keyboard mode, sound, vibration, motion, practice records, consent state, and the locally cached Pro entitlement remain in the app's private storage.
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics may process a Firebase installation identifier and limited technical or content-free usage events, such as app version, platform, locale, screen or practice flow, advertisement state, and purchase flow outcome. Typed text is not attached to these events.
  • Crash diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics may process crash traces, installation and session identifiers, app version, operating-system and device information, and related diagnostics. Typed text is not intentionally attached to crash reports.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may process an advertising identifier, IP address and IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, consent choices, and advertisement impressions, clicks, or other interaction data. Typing Sprint uses banner, app-open, and interstitial ad formats; it does not offer rewarded ads.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes the optional one-time Pro purchase. WittyApps does not receive your card number or payment credentials. The app receives the product identifier and purchase result needed to unlock Pro.

How Information Is Used

Local data is used to calculate practice results, remember settings, and restore records and Pro status. Limited analytics and crash information is used to understand reliability and improve the app. AdMob uses advertising data to serve, secure, limit, and measure ads in the free version. WittyApps does not sell personal information or use it for credit, employment, insurance, or similar eligibility decisions.

Storage and Retention

Practice content, results, and preferences remain in the app's private storage until you clear the app's data or uninstall it, subject to Android backup behavior. WittyApps keeps no server copy of this local data. Google retains analytics, crash, advertising, consent, and purchase records under its applicable policies and legal obligations.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for limited usage measurement and stability diagnostics. Firebase privacy and security
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and regional consent choices. How Google uses information from apps
  • Google Play Billing for the optional one-time Pro purchase and restoration. Google Payments privacy notice
  • Google Play services for store version checks and the native review prompt.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Clear local data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored practice results and settings, subject to Android backup behavior.
  • Use the privacy options shown in the app to review or withdraw advertising consent where available.
  • Reset or delete your Android advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings.
  • Buy Pro once to remove advertising; Google Play keeps the purchase record needed for restoration.
  • Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection concerning personal information. Contact us, and we will help identify the appropriate provider process where Google controls the record.

Data Security

Android protects app-private local storage, and the listed third-party services use encrypted network connections. We limit custom analytics to content-free operational events. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Typing Sprint is a general-audience practice tool and is not directed to children under 13. WittyApps does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app, a service provider, or a legal requirement changes. The current version will remain at this address with a revised last-updated date.
